# Approver

You give the final approval before filing.

1. **Approval Queue** lists returns the client has confirmed.
2. Open the return; check the client's confirmation (in the portal, with time and IP, or recorded on their behalf with the method and proof).
3. **Approve**. The portal stores a fingerprint of the figures; if any figure changes later, the approval is cancelled and the return goes back to review.

You cannot approve a return you prepared yourself.
