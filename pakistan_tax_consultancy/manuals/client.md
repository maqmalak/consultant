# Client guide

## Your account

Create your account on the website (**Create account**), or open the link your consultant sent you on WhatsApp or by email and set a password. Verify your mobile number and email when the portal asks; you can tick **Send me updates on WhatsApp** in your profile.

## Your return, step by step

The home page shows your return as a chain of steps, with what is needed from you now.

1. **Questionnaire**: answer the yes / no questions (salary, business, rent, bank profit, property, vehicle…). The answers decide which documents you need to share.
2. **Documents**: upload each document on the list as a PDF or a clear photo (JPG / PNG). A document marked *Upload again* was not readable or not the right one; the reason is shown under it. You can also send documents to the firm's WhatsApp number; your consultant attaches them for you.
3. **Messages**: write to your consultant at any time; you see their replies here and on WhatsApp.
4. **Confirm your return**: when the return is ready you will be asked to check the figures (income, tax, refund or payable) and **Confirm**. If something is wrong, ask a question instead; the return goes back to your consultant with your comment.
5. **Filed**: once filed on FBR IRIS you see the acknowledgment number and can download the FBR receipt from **Vault**.

## Payments

**Payments** shows the fee for each service and the firm's bank and wallet details. Pay by transfer, then upload the proof (screenshot or receipt). The firm checks it against its bank statement; the status changes to *Verified* and your invoice appears.

## Your vault

**Vault** keeps everything in one place: the documents you shared, the letters the firm prepared for you (engagement letter, authority letter, tax computation), your IRIS acknowledgments and invoices.

## Your data

Only you and the staff working on your return can see your records. Your CNIC is stored securely and shown masked.
