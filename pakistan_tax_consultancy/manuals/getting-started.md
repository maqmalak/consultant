# Getting started

## What the portal is for

The VVS portal is where the whole firm works on clients' income tax returns, and where clients follow their own return. Every return moves through the same steps, and every step is done by one role:

| Step | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The client is registered (office, website, site visit or Excel import) | Consultant, Field Officer, Admin | Clients, Site Visits, Import from Excel |
| Documents are collected and checked | Client, Consultant, Data Entry | Verify Documents, Document Vault |
| The return is prepared and calculated | Consultant, Data Entry | In Preparation |
| The return is reviewed | Reviewer | Review Queue |
| The client confirms the figures | Client (portal), or staff on their behalf | Client Confirmation |
| The return is approved | Approver (never the preparer) | Approval Queue |
| The return is filed on IRIS and the acknowledgment recorded | Filing Officer | Filing Queue, Awaiting Acknowledgment |

## Signing in

Open the portal, sign in with your work email and password. The left menu shows only what your role may use; the **queues** show only the returns waiting for you. Your clients are the ones assigned to you; admins see every client.

## The screens you will use most

- **Dashboard**: counts of your clients, returns, documents to verify, overdue returns and follow-ups, with charts.
- **Clients**: search by name, CNIC, NTN, mobile, email or client code (`C-00456`). A client's page shows their returns, documents, fees and messages.
- **Queues**: one list per step; open a return from there.
- **Document Vault**: a client's files as folders `C-00456 · Name / TY2026 / document type`, letters on the firm letterhead, IRIS receipts and invoices.
- **Search** (`Ctrl K`) jumps to any client, return or page.
- **Ask VVS** (`Ctrl J`), if switched on for you: ask questions about your clients and returns in plain language.

## Rules that protect the firm and the client

- A figure changed after review or approval sends the return back to review and cancels the approval.
- The person who prepared a return can never approve it.
- A return is shown as filed only with an IRIS acknowledgment number.
- Every download of a client file and every status change is written to the audit log.
- Calculators and tax computations are **estimates** until the reviewer has checked them against the Finance Act.
