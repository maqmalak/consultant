# Data entry

You enter what the client gives the firm, on their behalf, so the consultant can prepare the return.

## What you can do

- **What the client told us**: type the client's information from a call, a visit or WhatsApp into the return.
- **Questionnaire**: answer it for the client.
- **Attach documents** the client sent on WhatsApp or brought in person: on the return, **Attach from WhatsApp**, or **Document Vault → Client folders → Add files** with *Received via* set.
- **Record the client's approval** when they approve by phone, WhatsApp, in person or email: choose the method, add a note and, if you have it, the proof (screenshot). A colleague other than the one who entered the figures must record it (four-eyes rule).

## What you cannot do

- Verify documents. That is the consultant's check.
- Edit a return after it has been **Submitted to FBR**. Changes made after the client confirmed are flagged for the reviewer.

## Tips

- Search the client by CNIC, mobile or client code with `Ctrl K`.
- Use the client's own words in *What the client told us*; the consultant decides how it goes into the return.
