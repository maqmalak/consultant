# Field officer

You meet people in the market and in their offices, and bring them in as clients.

1. **Site Visits → New visit**: the person's name, business, mobile, what they need, and the location (allow the phone's location).
2. Set a **follow-up date**; it appears on your dashboard and in the CRM.
3. When the person agrees, **Convert to client** on the visit; the consultant takes it from there.

Every visit also creates a CRM lead, so the firm can follow it up by WhatsApp or phone.
