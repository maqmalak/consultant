# Reviewer

You check every return before the client sees it.

## The review

1. **Review Queue** → open a return in *Pending Review* → **Start review**.
2. Go through the **review checklist**: each item *Pass*, *Fail* or *N/A*. Compare the income and withholding with the documents in the vault.
3. Check the calculation against the slabs of that tax year.
4. Either **Request changes** with a comment (the return goes back to the consultant), or **Mark reviewed** when every item passed.
5. **Send to client**: the client is asked to confirm in their portal. The client needs a portal login.

## Good to know

- A change to any figure after your review sends the return back to *Pending Review* automatically.
- Your comments are kept on the return's history and the client never sees internal notes.
