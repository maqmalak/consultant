# Tax consultant

You register clients, collect and check their documents, and prepare their returns. You see the clients assigned to you.

## Register a client

**Clients → New client**. Enter the name and type; CNIC, NTN, mobile and email are checked for duplicates as you type. The client gets a permanent **client code** (`C-00456`). On the client page, **Invite to portal** creates the login and gives you a link to send on WhatsApp or by email.

Many clients at once: **Import from Excel** (see that guide).

## Start a return

On the client page, **Open return** for the tax year. The document checklist is created from the client type and the questionnaire answers; the return starts in **Documents Pending**.

## Documents

- **Verify Documents** lists uploads waiting for you. Open each one, then **Verify** or **Reject** with a reason the client will read.
- A file sent on WhatsApp: on the return, **Attach from WhatsApp** and choose the message; it is marked *received via WhatsApp*.
- **Document Vault → client → Client folders** shows the files as `C-00456 · Name / TY2026 / type`; **Add files** uploads several at once, each one waiting for verification.

When the last required document is verified, the return moves to **Draft** by itself.

## Prepare

On the return:

1. **What the client told us**: the notes from the client meeting or call.
2. **Questionnaire**: on the client's behalf if they have not answered.
3. Income sources, deductions, withholding tax and advance tax lines.
4. **Calculate**. The computation is an estimate until review.
5. **Submit for review**.

If the reviewer asks for changes, the return comes back in **Changes Required** with their comment; fix it and **Resume preparation**.

## After filing

The client sees the acknowledgment in their portal. Next year, start a new return from the client page; last year's figures stay in its history.
